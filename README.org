* Sleep analysis
Analyzes WHOOP export data to get a picture of how sleep, recovery, and training
interact over time. The script (~analyze_sleep.py~) reads the CSV files from a
WHOOP data export, crunches the numbers, and spits out a self-contained HTML
report (~sleep_report.html~) with embedded charts.

It auto-detects the latest ~my_whoop_data_*~ directory in the ~health/~ folder,
so after a fresh export you just unzip and re-run.

** Running it
#+begin_src bash
cd ~/bme && source .venv/bin/activate
python health/analyze_sleep.py
#+end_src

Opens ~health/sleep_report.html~ in your browser to view.

** What's in the report
- *Summary statistics* — mean, median, min, max, std dev for sleep duration,
  performance, efficiency, SWS/REM percentages, sleep debt, respiratory rate,
  recovery, HRV, and resting heart rate
- *Sleep duration & performance* — nightly asleep hours vs sleep need, with
  performance % on a second axis and period averages
- *Sleep stages over time* — stacked area chart of SWS, REM, and light sleep
  minutes per night
- *SWS + REM proportions* — per-night bar chart with a target band for SWS
  (15-25%)
- *Bedtime & wake consistency* — scatter plot tracking when you went to bed and
  woke up, with average lines and hourly gridlines
- *Sleep debt trend* — how sleep debt accumulates and recovers over the period
- *Sleep efficiency* — time asleep vs time in bed, plotted against an 85% target
- *Recovery & HRV correlations* — scatter plots with trend lines showing how
  sleep performance, duration, and SWS relate to next-day recovery and HRV
- *Training load impact* — day strain and workout duration vs sleep quality, plus
  a workout days vs rest days comparison
- *Weekly averages* — smoothed weekly view of sleep hours, performance, recovery,
  and HRV trends
